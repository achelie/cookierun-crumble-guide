GingerCraven has a rude way of making a decent account look broken. You can enter with 292.71M Power, survive most of the fight, then watch the final knockback scatter the lineup and erase the attempt. Adding another damage dealer usually makes that ending arrive sooner.

The safer answer is a sustain shell built around Scorpion Cookie. Three healers keep the group upright, GingerBright cuts the boss's ATK, Dark Choco lowers DEF, and the rest of the formation turns each Scorpion cycle into useful damage. A clean opening still matters, so expect an occasional restart. Compared with a damage-heavy team, this plan gives the fight fewer ways to go wrong.

<GuideSection id="why-gingercraven-wipes-teams" title="Why GingerCraven wipes a strong team">

Two problems overlap here. First, GingerCraven hits hard enough that a normal stage team can lose one support before its healing loop settles. Second, a large gap below the stage's Recommended Power can reduce the damage your Cookies deal. That leaves the boss alive longer, so the lineup has to absorb more dangerous cycles.

This is why the Power number can feel dishonest. The formation may have the right buffs and debuffs but still need a little more account strength. Check the [CookieRun: Crumble Power guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-power-guide-stage-damage/) if your damage suddenly falls off at this stage. A small Resolve or promotion gain can matter more than replacing a Cookie that already performs the correct job.

The other trap is movement. When the team's HP is low, dragging everyone across the arena can walk the Cookies into another boss skill before Grapevine, Lime, or Herb finishes a cast. Stop. Let the green bars recover, then reposition.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="cookie-jobs" title="Know what each Cookie is doing">

Scorpion is the main damage dealer and should be the leader. Her damage over time becomes dependable at five stars, where the relevant trigger reaches 100%. If she is below that breakpoint, the team can still work, but failed applications create wider swings between attempts.

GingerBright and Dark Choco prepare the target. GingerBright applies ATK Down, which reduces the pressure on every healer. Dark Choco brings DEF Down now that Devil Cookie is less attractive for this job. Pomegranate adds Skill Amp Up so Scorpion and the support cycle get more value from each cast.

Grapevine, Lime, and Herb form the healing core. Grapevine is the plain, reliable healer. Lime adds healing and CRIT Chance Up, which lets the team survive without abandoning burst damage. Herb gives another steady recovery line and receives Multi-strike, so the same synergy package can improve his healing output.

Macaron and Skating Queen turn that survival time into damage. Macaron raises CRIT Chance, while Skating Queen raises CRIT DMG. Above 100% CRIT Chance, attacks can double crit; above 200%, they can triple crit. Reaching those numbers is hard, but the two buffs still make every Scorpion cycle less dependent on one lucky roll.

Tiger Lily supplies a useful low-rarity body and Multi-shot synergy. Nameless Cake Hound reduces damage taken. Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician brings healing plus ATK Up. None of these slots looks glamorous on a damage chart, which is exactly why the lineup survives long enough to finish the boss.

If you are deciding where Scorpion and the supports sit in the broader roster, use the [CookieRun: Crumble Tier List](/tier-list/) as context. Promotion and Sugar Runes still decide whether your version can do the advertised job.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="sugar-rune-priorities" title="Build the Sugar Runes for the job">

Do not give every support the same Rune page just because Skill Amp appears on many recommendations. The recent stat changes narrowed where Skill Amp helps debuffs.

- Scorpion wants Skill Haste, Skill Amp, and ATK Amp. Keep enough Focus for her damage plan to land reliably, but do not reroll a strong set only to chase a prettier number.
- Grapevine, Lime, Tiger Lily, Macaron, Herb, Skating Queen, and Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician prefer Skill Haste and Skill Amp, followed by useful ATK or ATK Amp rolls.
- GingerBright wants Focus, Accuracy, or defensive stats. Skill Amp no longer strengthens the ATK Down effect.
- Dark Choco needs Focus for DEF Down and enough defense to stay present through the dangerous cycles.
- Nameless Cake Hound values Skill Haste plus defensive rolls. A dead damage reducer is decorative furniture.
- Pomegranate wants Skill Haste and Skill Amp so the team receives the support buff more often.

Upgrade the SR and SSR lines you can afford before chasing perfect rolls. A workable set across all twelve Cookies beats one support with museum-quality Sugar Runes and eleven underbuilt teammates.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="pet-loadout" title="Use the three-Pet survival package">

Octo Wasabi grants Duration synergy and raises ATK for Cookies that receive it. Holy Baby Drop gives ATK to all allies. Majestic King Choco Drop raises Max HP, buying the extra second that often separates a recovery cast from a wipe.

You can clear without Majestic King Choco Drop. Use Kiwi Bird for DEF Up if you do not own the SSR Pet. That replacement lowers the team's HP cushion but keeps the same purpose: survive the knockback with enough Cookies alive to restart the healing loop.

Avoid swapping the defensive Pet for a pure damage option until the formation already survives consistently. Faster failure is still failure.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="fight-sequence" title="Play the knockback without panicking">

1. Make Scorpion the leader before entering. Her damage over time finishes the boss, so the other eleven slots support that cycle.
2. Collapse the group close to GingerCraven when the opening allows it. Stacking keeps the formation's movement predictable and helps the support cycle cover the same area.
3. Watch HP before dragging the leader. If several bars are low, wait for Grapevine, Lime, or Herb to cast. Moving during that gap can feed the team directly into the next skill.
4. Expect the boss to push the formation away. Survive that moment first. Once the team stabilizes, move back in and let Scorpion continue stacking damage.
5. Restart when GingerBright, Grapevine, or another core support dies before the main push. The team has enough sustain to win, but it cannot repair a broken cycle with half its parts missing.

The retry rule matters because this setup does not work every time. Boss targeting, early damage, and a missed debuff can create a bad attempt even when the account has enough Power. Give the formation a few clean openings before spending resources on a panic rebuild.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="replacement-checklist" title="Change one thing after a failed run">

Start with the death log in your head. If the boss survives while the team remains healthy, raise Scorpion's damage, promotion, or Power. If supports die before Scorpion can work, improve their defensive Runes or use Kiwi Bird. If the boss's damage spikes because ATK Down fails, add Focus or Accuracy to GingerBright rather than piling more Skill Amp onto the wrong stat.

Change one slot or Rune page, then retry. Replacing three Cookies at once creates a new team and teaches you nothing about the old failure. The same rule applies to movement: wait for the heal, make one clean reposition, and see whether the formation reaches the next cycle intact.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="gingercraven-faq" title="GingerCraven boss FAQ">

### What is the best team for GingerCraven in CookieRun: Crumble?

Use Macaron, Pomegranate, Grapevine, Lime, Nameless Cake Hound, Dark Choco, Scorpion, Skating Queen, Tiger Lily, Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician, Herb, and GingerBright. Pair them with Octo Wasabi, Holy Baby Drop, and Majestic King Choco Drop.

### Who should be the leader against GingerCraven?

Scorpion Cookie should lead. Her damage over time is the main finisher, and the other eleven Cookies protect or amplify that plan.

### Why does the correct GingerCraven team still lose?

A large Power gap can reduce your damage, and one early support death can break the healing cycle. Restart a bad opening, then raise account Power or defensive Runes if the same Cookie keeps dying.

### Do I need Majestic King Choco Drop for GingerCraven?

No. Majestic King Choco Drop's Max HP is excellent for this fight, but Kiwi Bird can replace it with DEF Up when you need a free defensive option.

### Should I use GingerBrave or GingerBright for ATK Down?

Use GingerBright. GingerBright applies the ATK Down effect used by this formation, while GingerBrave does not fill that exact role.

</GuideSection>
