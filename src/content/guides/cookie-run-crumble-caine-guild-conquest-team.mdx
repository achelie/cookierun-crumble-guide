<GuideSection id="cheesecake-swap" title="Replace Cheesecake and keep Milk">

If you already run a similar team with Cheesecake, make that one replacement first. Caine takes Cheesecake's place. Milk stays. Changing both would remove the pairing you came to test and leave you with a result that answers a different question.

Caine works well alongside Milk's penetration and the wider lineup. Treat that as the reason to test their overlap, without assigning a made-up damage multiplier to it. A cleaner opening is encouraging, but the completed fight tells you whether the swap helps your account.

Save your Cheesecake preset before editing it. Give the Caine version the same attackers, Pets, and other settings for the comparison. If you upgrade Rye between attempts or change three supports together, you lose track of which change raised the score.

Players still building the older core can use the [Piñata team guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-guild-conquest-team-guide/) for Wind Archer and Milky Way alternatives. Those are separate starting points. This formation already includes Brightseeker and Tea Knight, so copying only its Caine slot does not give a smaller roster the whole team.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="caine-stars" title="Try three-star Caine before chasing five stars">

Three stars is a reasonable point to put Caine through a trial. The recommendation extends below five stars, but it does not establish a universal minimum or prove that every low-star copy beats a fully built Cheesecake. Your other Cookies still have to do their jobs.

Start with the Caine you own. Let him run beside Milk and compare the finished score with your saved preset. If the team improves consistently, you have an account-specific reason to keep using him. If it does not, inspect the rest of the setup before spending more resources on a star target.

Check the [Cookie tier list](/tier-list/) when deciding which character deserves your next upgrade across several modes. For this particular slot, keep a record of the Guild Conquest result too. A general ranking cannot tell you whether your current Caine build fits your current Milk setup.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="caine-pets" title="Keep the three Pets for the first comparison">

Use Hot Doggie, Octo Wasabi, and Holy Baby Drop. Copy the Pet identities before adjusting their upgrades or trying substitutes. Keep a note of each Pet's level with your saved preset, so that a later upgrade does not get mixed into the Cookie comparison.

Hot Doggie supplies Skill Amp Up. Holy Baby Drop raises allied ATK. Octo Wasabi grants Duration synergy and raises ATK for its receivers. These effects help explain why the Pet package belongs with the formation. Replacing one also changes the support your Cookies receive.

Keep the trio steady while comparing Cheesecake and Caine. Once you choose the stronger Cookie preset, you can test a Pet change on its own. Otherwise, a better result might come from the new Pet, and you would credit Caine for an improvement you never isolated.

<GuideSection id="caine-stat-presets" title="Compare the two stat presets fairly">

Two approaches are worth comparing: Skill Amp with ATK and some Skill Haste, or a setup focused on Skill Amp and Skill Haste. There is no confirmed winning ratio here. Neither approach comes with a reliable percentage split you should copy onto every Cookie.

Skill Amp supports eligible skill effects. Skill Haste affects how often skills return, while ATK contributes another offensive stat. Individual skills use those stats differently, so the team needs more judgment than selecting the same three labels twelve times. The [gear and Sugar Rune stat guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-gear-sugar-rune-stats-guide/) explains the underlying choices.

Begin with the preset closest to your current equipment. Save it, run it, and write down the final damage. Then switch to your Amp-and-Haste version without replacing Cookies or Pets. Compare under the same boss conditions so that the stat change remains the main difference.

Avoid stripping useful ATK rolls just to make the second preset look tidy. You are testing a direction, and your available gear may favor one version heavily. Keep the old preset recoverable. A theory about cast frequency is a poor excuse to destroy the setup that already earns your guild a dependable score.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="test-caine-damage" title="Watch the opening, then record the finish">

Caine can make the damage counter look livelier almost immediately. Use the opening to check that you loaded the intended preset and that the team begins normally. Do not stop there. An early lead can disappear if important Cookies fall or their later casts fail to line up well.

Run each version three times as a practical starting test. Record every final result, along with any early death. This is a comparison method, not a game requirement. If one setup posts a huge outlier and two weak attempts, give it another pass before calling it your new default.

Keep your notes short enough that you will actually use them:

* Preset name and whether it uses Cheesecake or Caine.
* Final damage for each attempt.
* Caine's stars and the stat approach you changed.
* Any early death or obvious interruption.

Compare the ordinary results as well as the personal best. You want to know whether the swap helps repeatedly, and whether its best run is worth pursuing. One exciting opening does not answer either question by itself.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="level-tuning" title="Leave exact level tuning until the team works">

There is no confirmed Cookie-by-Cookie level recipe for this Caine formation. Avoid copying a precise low-level support setup just because someone says it raises damage. A specific recommendation needs a clear explanation of which effect changes and a repeatable score comparison.

First get the complete formation working with your existing levels. Then compare the Caine swap and the two stat directions. Level experiments belong after those checks, when you can return to a saved baseline and tell whether the adjustment helped.

If your score disappoints, check the roster against both rows. Grapevine occupies the first bottom slot, and Tea Knight occupies the last. Neither is a spare portrait. Also check the exact Milk variant: this preset uses Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician. Similar names make copying a team surprisingly easy to get wrong.

When the same Cookie dies early across attempts, address that survival problem before interpreting a low score as proof against Caine. When the team survives and the score stays flat, return to the saved Cheesecake preset for a direct comparison. Keep the stronger result while you investigate the next change.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="caine-faq" title="Caine Guild Conquest FAQ">

### Who does Caine replace in Guild Conquest?

Caine replaces Cheesecake in this setup. Keep the other eleven Cookies, including Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician, steady while testing the change. Save the Cheesecake version so you can compare the two without rebuilding it from memory.

### Can I use Caine at three stars?

Yes, three-star Caine is worth testing here. Five stars is not a prerequisite for trying the swap. His result still depends on your roster and builds, so compare completed attempts before committing more upgrade materials.

### Which Pets go with the Caine team?

Use Hot Doggie, Octo Wasabi, and Holy Baby Drop. Keep all three unchanged during your first Cookie comparison so a Pet swap does not confuse the result. Record their current levels before making later upgrades.

### Is Skill Amp and Skill Haste always the better preset?

No confirmed winner applies to every account. Compare Skill Amp, ATK, and some Skill Haste against the Amp-and-Haste approach with the same lineup and Pets. Keep the saved preset that produces stronger completed runs with your available equipment.
