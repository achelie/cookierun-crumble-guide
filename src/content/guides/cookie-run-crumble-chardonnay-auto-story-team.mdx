An auto team loses its appeal when you have to drag it back onto the boss every few stages. Bari and Cherry Cola now have a better chance of staying on the right target, and Chardonnay takes the support slot that Macaron held in earlier story setups. That gives this formation a useful job: push ordinary stages with fewer interruptions, then change the part that caused a difficult boss attempt to fail.

Start with the full twelve-Cookie lineup below. Keep a second Pet setup ready for knock-ups, and resist the urge to replace half the team after one loss. This is a developed roster, so the names alone will not reproduce its progress on a fresh account.

<GuideSection id="boss-targeting" title="Why Bari and Cherry Cola chase the boss more reliably">

Bari and Cherry Cola prioritize high-ATK enemies. Earlier boss encounters could send them after the extra enemies instead, leaving the boss alive while your strongest melee attackers worked through the wrong targets.

The targeting rule still favors high ATK. In the adjusted story encounters, bosses have higher ATK than their accompanying enemies. That gives these two Cookies a clearer route to the boss. That matters because another damage upgrade cannot help much when the attacker keeps hitting something else.

Try the updated formation on auto before carrying over an old positioning workaround. Watch where Bari and Cherry Cola go once the boss and extra enemies appear. If they reach the boss but the team still loses, check survival, interruptions, or overall damage next.

This improvement does not remove every obstacle from automatic progression. A Cookie can select the right target and still spend the fight airborne. That is where the Pet change earns its place.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="knock-up-pet-swap" title="Replace Chargemellow when knock-ups stop the team">

The default Pets cover three jobs. Hot Doggie adds Skill Amp, Holy Baby Drop raises allied ATK, and Chargemellow supports Multi-strike users. Keep this setup for ordinary stages and bosses that let the team keep attacking.

**Against a knock-up boss, replace Chargemellow with Candy Shade Pouch.** Keep Hot Doggie and Holy Baby Drop. The pouch gives the squad Knock-up Resistance and additional HP. Cool Mint and the hammer-wielding Choco Werehound boss are examples where this adjustment deserves an early test.

You give up Chargemellow's Multi-strike contribution in exchange for more time spent attacking, buffing, and recovering. Compare the whole attempt. A smaller damage bonus can produce a better clear when the alternative leaves your support Cookies unable to cast.

Chardonnay's Knockback Resistance and the pouch's Knock-up Resistance address different disruptions. Do not assume that adding Chardonnay makes every control-focused Pet unnecessary. Keep the pouch preset ready and switch when the boss repeatedly lifts your team.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="bari-replacements" title="Replace Bari according to the reason you are stuck">

A low-promotion Bari can feel weaker than the name suggests. You can still test the team while building her, but you also have three practical replacement paths.

Scorpion is a damage alternative when Bari is missing or underbuilt. Make that swap in Bari's slot and leave the rest of the preset alone for the first comparison. It gives you a manageable test instead of a completely different lineup.

Wind Archer is another option when your copy has much stronger promotion. A highly promoted TSSR can contribute substantial team Power. Story damage depends partly on the team's Power relative to the stage, so the replacement can help beyond its own attacks. The [story Power guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-power-guide-stage-damage/) explains why that comparison matters.

Choose Moon Rabbit when staying alive is the problem. If Bari is unavailable, Moon Rabbit can take her place. If Bari is already working, replace Dark Choco instead. That second swap removes a DEF Down slot, so judge whether the extra protection makes up for the lost damage support.

Do not make all three changes together. A surviving team that times out needs a different response from one that collapses before its first recovery cycle.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="chardonnay-runes" title="Build Chardonnay for the support slot">

Chardonnay is the default support choice in this preset, replacing Macaron rather than joining alongside her. Chardonnay brings a CRIT Chance buff, Knockback Resistance, and Multi-shot synergy support. Use this slot to support the rest of the formation, including the attackers who need help staying on the boss.

Skill Amp is the recommended Sugar Rune direction for Chardonnay. Keep the focus on the support contribution before chasing a set of personal damage stats. A usable build lets you test the formation sooner; the team does not require a perfect collection of SSR rolls before its first attempt.

Use the [Sugar Rune stat guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-gear-sugar-rune-stats-guide/) when the rest of the roster needs work. Copying the Cookie list should not mean giving every Cookie the same Rune set. A fragile Charge attacker and a support buffer have different reasons for failing.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="promotion-crystal-priorities" title="Set promotion targets before spending crystals">

Higher promotions help, but you do not need to wait until every slot matches a finished account. Try the formation at your current investment, identify the weak point, and work toward useful milestones.

Chardonnay and Moon Rabbit have five-star targets in this progression route. After those initial goals, further Chardonnay investment toward ten stars becomes a longer project if your budget allows it. Continue building Bari when she is part of your story plan, without treating a poorly performing low-star copy as the only available choice.

The [tier list](/tier-list/) helps compare these investments with the rest of your roster. Check where a Cookie fits, then ask whether more copies will improve the team you actually use. Another promotion on a useful support may solve more fights than raising a specialist you rarely field.

Favor the relevant Cookie pickup banners for crystal spending. Regular premium pulls miss the pickup-event benefits, while Pet pulls do not advance the Cookie-promotion requirements behind Stellar Link. Cookie promotions feed that account progression, which gives the choice a benefit beyond one lineup.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="dimensional-rift-goal" title="Finish story with the Dimensional Rift in mind">

Reaching the end of the current story opens the route into Dimensional Rift. Its seasonal exchange and progression rewards add a reason to push rather than leave an almost-finished chapter sitting untouched.

Check the current exchange and milestone rewards when you arrive. If you cannot reach the content before the season ends, finishing more story still prepares the account for the next opening. Do not turn a closing season into an excuse to empty every crystal balance.

Keep both Pet setups saved as you work through the remaining stages. If a Cookie replacement gets you past a wall, note which problem it solved before carrying that change into every later fight.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="auto-stage-checklist" title="Test one difficult stage before leaving auto running">

1. Copy all twelve Cookies and the default three Pets. Keep Milk as captain for the initial test.
2. Watch Bari and Cherry Cola when the boss appears. Confirm that they engage the boss instead of spending the attempt on accompanying enemies.
3. If repeated knock-ups interrupt the team, swap Chargemellow for Candy Shade Pouch and repeat that stage.
4. If the team still dies, try Moon Rabbit in Dark Choco's slot, or in Bari's slot when Bari is missing or underbuilt.
5. If everyone survives but the timer runs out, compare Scorpion or your better-promoted Wind Archer with Bari before buying another upgrade.

Once the changed setup clears the problem stage, return to auto and watch the next boss transition. Save the successful variant so the next interruption takes one preset change instead of another round of rebuilding.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="chardonnay-story-faq" title="Chardonnay auto story team FAQ">

### Can this team clear every story boss automatically?

Treat it as a story baseline. Promotion, Runes, team Power, and the boss mechanic still affect the result. Candy Shade Pouch helps with knock-ups, and Moon Rabbit offers a survival alternative when the default formation fails.

### Who replaces Bari if I do not own her?

Try Scorpion for damage, a strongly promoted Wind Archer for damage and team Power, or Moon Rabbit when you need protection. Keep the other eleven slots steady while comparing the replacement.

### Does Chardonnay replace Macaron in this formation?

Yes. Chardonnay takes Macaron's support slot in this preset. Favor Skill Amp for Chardonnay, keep the remaining support group, and test the team before changing more members.

### Which Pet should I remove for Candy Shade Pouch?

Remove Chargemellow. Keep Hot Doggie and Holy Baby Drop, then test Candy Shade Pouch against repeated knock-ups. Return to the default trio when that protection no longer helps the fight.

</GuideSection>
