Some CookieRun: Crumble accounts play every day and still grow like they are dragging an Oven uphill. The problem is often the order of the work. Claim a Lucky Reward before pushing a stage, repeat a comfortable dungeon floor, leave Arena tickets capped, and the account keeps buying tomorrow's upgrades at yesterday's prices.

You do not need a perfect roster to fix that. You need each daily action to make the next one pay more.

<GuideSection id="push-before-claiming" title="Push stages before claiming the good rewards">

Campaign progress improves more than the chapter number. Lucky Rewards scale with the stage milestones shown in their reward panel, so check that panel before spending the day's rolls. If the next milestone is one or two clears away, make the push first. The same roll can then land on a better reward table.

Do not get cute near reset. A saved roll that expires is worth less than an early roll on the current table. Push while the team still has a realistic clear, then collect before the daily cutoff.

This also applies to idle rewards. A boss sitting at 5% health is a reason to collect, upgrade, and retry. A boss deleting the front line in five seconds is a team problem. Start from a proven shell on the [recommended teams page](/teams/), then change the slot that is failing instead of feeding materials to all twelve Cookies at once.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="enhancement-order" title="Use +5, +10, and +15 as different jobs">

Early account enhancement does not need a grand strategy. Bring the available lines to +5, then raise them evenly toward +10. Those cheap levels spread useful stats across the roster and keep one expensive node from eating the whole coin pile.

The choice matters more at +15. Accuracy is the safest first target because a missed attack contributes nothing. Focus comes next for a lineup that depends on DEF Down, damage over time, or another debuff landing reliably. After those, choose Skill Amp or ATK based on what the active Cookies use. CRIT, Damage Reduction, and CRIT Resist can follow.

If the team dies early, move a defensive upgrade forward. If it survives and loses to the timer, stop buying comfort and add damage. The menu order is not a commandment.

<GuideTip title="A useful +15 order">

For a normal stage-pushing account, start with Accuracy, then Focus, then Skill Amp or ATK. Change that order only when repeated attempts show a specific survival problem.

</GuideTip>

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="gear-tie-breakers" title="Take the Power upgrade, then read the substats">

During the first stretch, replacing gear with the higher displayed Power is fine. Those pieces will not stay forever, and crossing a stage Power band can matter more than a tidy substat on weak gear.

When two pieces are close, use Accuracy as the first tie-breaker, followed by Focus, Skill Amp, and Skill Haste. This is a progression rule, not a forever build. Once better gear has several useful rolls, match the stats to the Cookie's actual job.

Do not keep swapping blindly once the green number stops helping. The [Power and stage damage guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-power-guide-stage-damage/) explains why a higher total can rescue one run yet lose another after you break an important synergy.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="push-dungeon-floors" title="Stop farming the easy Daily Dungeon floor">

Daily Dungeon keys become much better when you push first. The gap is not small. A lower floor can show about 64 of a reward, while a late floor can reach roughly 6,000 per key. Spending today's key on the old floor also delays the permanent upgrade that could clear tomorrow's floor.

Use the first attempt on the highest uncleared stage that looks reasonable. If it nearly clears, take the cheapest available account upgrade and try again. Farm the previous floor only after the push has genuinely stopped.

Crumble Dungeon deserves at least one daily run too. Plaque Tower can wait when it hard-walls the account, but a fresh dungeon floor and its first-clear reward should not sit untouched because the older one finishes ten seconds faster.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="spend-arena-tickets" title="Turn Arena tickets into permanent stats">

New players sometimes avoid Arena until their roster looks respectable. Meanwhile the ticket counter fills and stops producing attempts. Pick opponents you are likely to beat and spend the tickets.

Fame levels raise account stats. They also build bonuses such as boss damage, elemental damage, and CRIT damage. Those gains follow you back into PvE, which makes a safe Arena win part of stage progression.

Lineup Power is the number to watch during a stage. In one simple test, Wind Archer's hit read 192 on the displayed scale. Adding a healer with no ATK buff and no new Pet moved the hit to 576. The healer did not secretly become a damage support. The larger lineup crossed into a better Power correction band.

That is why total account Power can look impressive while the selected formation still struggles. Spend Arena attempts for permanent growth, but judge the next stage with the twelve Cookies you actually brought.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="save-stellar-points" title="Accept a good Stellar Link board early">

Stellar Points are too scarce for perfection on a young account. Around 70% coverage is a sensible stopping point for a free player. Keep the board, move deeper into the system, and save the serious rerolls for later sections where the percentages are larger.

Locking a section also raises the cost of future attempts. It is easy to spend a week's currency fixing one ugly gap while gaining less Power than a cheap research node would have given immediately. The [account resource traps guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-resource-guide-account-traps/) covers the other taps that look harmless until the balance hits zero.

Cookie promotions feed Stellar Link progress, which gives featured banners a second job. Five stars is a practical baseline for a pickup Cookie you expect to use. Beyond that, read the next promotion effect and check the current [CookieRun: Crumble Tier List](/tier-list/). Six or seven stars should solve a real roster need, not soothe the discomfort of stopping mid-banner.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="research-and-guild" title="Climb research and use the guild exchange">

Move up the research tree one required node at a time. Filling every early branch feels organized, but it delays account-wide upgrades and burns the materials needed higher up. Research speed is a strong early pick because every later timer benefits. The side chest research near Lucky Rewards can wait while supplies are tight.

Join a guild before your roster looks impressive. Guild membership works across servers, and you can find your server's S-number under Settings, then Account. Guild Conquest rewards, ranking rewards, research help, and the exchange all speed up an account that would otherwise play alone.

If you do not own Tea Knight, save guild currency for the first useful copy. His Boss DMG Up job is difficult to replace in Guild Conquest. After securing him, Stellar Points become a strong repeat purchase for free and light-spending accounts because their regular income is thin.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="daily-growth-route" title="Run this 15-minute growth route">

1. Check the next Lucky Reward milestone, then push the campaign before rolling.
2. Spend Daily Dungeon keys on first clears or the highest farmable floor.
3. Use Arena tickets on safe opponents before the counter caps.
4. Claim Fame, enhancement, collection, and other permanent account upgrades.
5. Advance one useful research path and collect guild rewards.
6. Keep a workable Stellar Link board instead of rerolling from boredom.
7. Return to the stage wall with the upgraded lineup.

One failed retry is information. A team that survives but times out needs damage or a better Power band. A formation that collapses immediately needs defense, healing, or a safer position. Once the sensible upgrades are spent, collect the remaining rewards and stop. An extra hour of menu shuffling does not create another dungeon key.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="fast-growth-faq" title="CookieRun: Crumble fast progression FAQ">

### Should I use Lucky Reward rolls before or after pushing stages?

Push first when the next reward milestone is within reach, then use the rolls on the improved table. Spend them before reset if the alternative is losing them.

### What should I enhance to +15 first?

Accuracy is the safest first choice for normal progression because missed attacks deal no damage. Follow with Focus when debuffs matter, then Skill Amp or ATK. Move defense forward only when the team dies early.

### Should I repeat an easy Daily Dungeon floor?

Try the highest uncleared floor first. Higher floors improve each key's payout, and first clears add more rewards. Repeat the previous floor only after the next one has genuinely stopped your lineup.

### How much Stellar Link coverage is enough for a beginner?

About 70% is a reasonable early stopping point for a free account. Later sections offer larger gains, while locking and rerolling the opening board can drain scarce Stellar Points.

### What should I buy first from the guild exchange?

Save for Tea Knight if you do not own him and plan to play Guild Conquest. After the first useful copy, Stellar Points are a strong recurring choice because dependable free sources are limited.

</GuideSection>
