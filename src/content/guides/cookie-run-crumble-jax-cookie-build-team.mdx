Jax Cookie can hit hard and lower an enemy's DEF, but a big skill number alone won't tell you whether he deserves your next Sugar Runes. Start with ATK Amp, put him beside a team that can use the debuff, and compare him with the Cookie he replaces. You can do that before chasing every promotion star.

Jax is a Fire Charge Cookie who receives Duration synergy. His bat deals damage and pushes enemies back; his grenade targets a high-ATK enemy, damages nearby opponents, and applies DEF Down. The combination makes him an interesting choice for a boss team that already has other damage dealers ready to hit the same target.

The early battle used a two-star Jax without Sugar Runes in a strong team. That is enough to make the kit worth testing, though it does not establish a best-in-slot build or a guaranteed score. Use the full lineup below as a starting point and measure the swap on your own account.

<GuideSection id="jax-skill" title="Understand the bat and grenade">

Jax Maxing has two useful pieces. The bat swings and pushes enemies while dealing ATK-based damage. The grenade aims at a high-ATK enemy, hits the surrounding area, and reduces DEF. The attack can help the rest of the team during the debuff window, provided your damage dealers are still alive and attacking the target.

One early skill display showed 541% of ATK for the bat, 52.5% of ATK for the grenade, a grenade range of 1.1, and 45% DEF Down for five seconds. Treat these as figures from that displayed setup, not permanent values for every promotion. Read your own skill panel when comparing upgrades.

The grenade's target selection deserves attention in fights with multiple enemies. If the high-ATK enemy is an add, Jax may throw the grenade there instead of where you wanted it. Watch the debuff indicator before assuming the boss received it. The bat's push can also change where enemies stand, so check whether your team can still reach them afterward.

Skill Amp does not increase the amount of the stat-reducing DEF Down debuff. That limit applies to the debuff itself. It does not establish that every part of the skill's damage ignores Skill Amp, so avoid throwing away useful offensive gear based on that assumption.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="jax-runes" title="Start Jax's Sugar Runes with ATK Amp">

ATK Amp is the first stat to try. The bat and grenade both list damage as a percentage of ATK, giving Jax a clear reason to want more of it. Use the best relevant rolls you already own before spending heavily on a perfect page.

CRIT Chance and CRIT DMG are reasonable offensive options to test alongside ATK Amp. Skill Haste is another possible choice if you want more frequent casts. These are starting ideas, not a measured stat ranking. There is no tested percentage split here, and the skill display on your own account should guide the choice.

Build two recoverable presets if your inventory allows it. Keep your strongest ATK-oriented page on one, then try a page with more critical stats or Skill Haste on the other. Change only Jax's page between attempts. A change to Pets, supports, or promotion at the same time makes the result harder to interpret.

Do not reroll a good page into the ground because every line fails to match a suggested label. A useful roll you already have can beat an expensive hunt for a theoretical ideal. The [Sugar Rune and gear guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-gear-sugar-rune-stats-guide/) explains how to weigh a stat page before spending resources.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="promotion" title="What extra stars add to Jax">

The promotion preview gives Jax a larger bat hit and a stronger DEF Down value. At five stars, the displayed bat damage was 598% of ATK, with 55% DEF Down and a bat push value of 120. A fully promoted preview showed 663% bat damage and 65% DEF Down. These are previewed skill values, not results from a fully built damage test.

You do not need to wait for those numbers to try him. The early battle used a two-star copy with no Sugar Runes. That makes a low-investment trial sensible if Jax already sits in your roster. It does not promise that a two-star Jax will outperform a highly promoted alternative.

Before spending for more copies, compare the team with your existing DEF Down Cookie. Look at your actual blocking point: is the team surviving but running out of damage, or losing a support too early? A higher debuff value cannot rescue a formation that falls apart before the damage dealers get to work.

The [Cookie tier list](/tier-list/) helps compare broader upgrade choices. For Jax, also keep the result of your own boss runs. A general ranking cannot account for the stars and equipment on both candidates in your roster.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="dark-choco-swap" title="Compare Jax with Dark Choco">

Dark Choco was the slot replaced for the initial event test. That gives you a useful one-for-one comparison if you already rely on him for DEF Down. Keep the other eleven Cookies, three Pets, and battle settings the same for your first attempts.

Run the Dark Choco version, save its result, then switch to Jax. Record whether the target receives the debuff, how long the fight lasts, and whether the team survives. Repeat the comparison a few times; a single unusually clean run can make either version look better than it is.

Do not treat the listed DEF Down percentages as a direct prediction of your final damage. The Cookies have different builds, promotions, attacks, and targeting. The enemy and the rest of the team also affect what you see. A larger displayed debuff is a reason to test the swap, not a damage multiplier you can safely write on the result screen.

If Jax loses, check whether his grenade reached the intended target and whether his Rune page is substantially weaker than Dark Choco's. Keep the stronger preset while you decide whether improving Jax is worth the materials.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="jax-pets" title="Keep the Pet package steady">

Octo Wasabi grants Duration synergy and ATK to its receivers. Jax receives Duration, making this Pet a natural fit for the displayed team. Caine and Milk contribute Duration too; check the active synergy in your own formation if you change either support.

Holy Baby Drop raises allied ATK, and Hot Doggie provides Skill Amp. Both support the wider team rather than asking Jax to carry the whole fight. Pomegranate also supplies Skill Amp Up, while Skating Queen adds CRIT DMG. Jax's DEF Down itself remains outside the Skill Amp increase described above.

Keep all three Pets equipped during the first Dark Choco comparison. If you change Octo Wasabi at the same time, you also change the synergy package. Test a Pet swap afterward, with the Cookie lineup fixed, so you can see which decision helped.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="battle-test" title="Test the team without wasting upgrades">

Start with a battle you can repeat under similar conditions. Save the old team, note its result, and try the Jax formation at your current promotion. The initial lineup was already strong, so its displayed Power should not be mistaken for a minimum requirement or a promise of the same clear on another account.

Watch three things during the attempt: where Jax throws the grenade, whether the intended target shows DEF Down, and whether your attackers survive long enough to use the opening. If a support dies before the fight develops, fix that problem before judging an offensive Rune swap.

Then add the ATK-oriented Rune page and repeat. Record clear time or final damage when the mode provides it, along with Jax's stars and any obvious early death. Compare several completed attempts. If the outcome improves consistently, you have a practical reason to keep the investment. If it does not, return to the saved setup and try a different stat page or target.

Jax may also be interesting in Arena because he targets a high-ATK enemy and brings DEF Down. That matchup still needs testing. The boss trial does not establish an Arena ranking, and an opponent who removes Jax early can make the result look very different from a longer PvE fight.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="jax-faq" title="Jax Cookie FAQ">

### What are the best Sugar Rune stats for Jax Cookie?

Start with ATK Amp and test CRIT Chance, CRIT DMG, or Skill Haste alongside it. These are early build options, not a confirmed best stat ratio. Compare saved Rune pages in the same fight before spending heavily on rerolls.

### Can Jax Cookie replace Dark Choco?

Jax can take Dark Choco's slot in a DEF Down comparison. Keep the other Cookies and Pets unchanged, then check the target's debuff and the completed result. Promotions, equipment, and enemy targeting can change which version works better for your account.

### Does Skill Amp increase Jax's DEF Down?

No. Skill Amp does not increase the amount of Jax's stat-reducing DEF Down debuff. That restriction should not be taken as proof that Skill Amp has no effect on every damage component of his skill.

### Is Jax worth using at two stars?

A two-star Jax without Sugar Runes can be tried in a strong team. Compare him with your current Cookie before investing more. The early trial does not prove that a two-star Jax will beat every highly promoted alternative.

</GuideSection>
