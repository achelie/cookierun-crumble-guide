A team clears several stages without trouble, then spends the next fight airborne. You add defense, reach another boss, and suddenly cannot deal enough damage. Rebuilding all twelve slots after every loss gets old fast.

Keep **three saved story presets**: a Princess Bari and Cherry Cola team for routine progression, an anti-knock-up team, and a GingerCraven team that keeps Power in mind. Redberry needs a separate adjustment because chasing that enemy can make the fight worse.

These are developed rosters. Copying the portraits will not copy the promotion and equipment behind them. If your Cookies have lower promotion or weaker equipment, use the survival adjustments below before judging the entire team. Finishing story opens access to the Dimensional Rift, so a reliable clear matters more than squeezing out a prettier damage chart.

<GuideSection id="choose-story-preset" title="Choose the preset from the failed fight">

Start with the Bari and Cherry Cola lineup for ordinary stages. Keep it while the team survives and finishes its targets. You do not need a specialized answer to every boss name.

Switch to the anti-knock-up setup when repeated lifts interrupt the formation. Hammer attacks and Cool Mint fights are the obvious cases. A support who cannot finish casting is not doing much supporting, however good the Rune page looks.

For GingerCraven, check whether your problem is incoming damage or damage that barely moves the boss's health bar. The third preset includes Wind Archer to help raise Power on an account where that slot offers a useful increase. You can use Cherry Cola in that slot if she works better on your account.

All three complete lineups also appear on the [Teams page](/teams/). Save them separately so you can return to a working setup after a failed experiment. Keep the Cookie and Pet order when you copy them; the numbered portraits make comparisons easier.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="story-survival-swaps" title="Fix survival without replacing the whole roster">

In the default Bari and Cherry Cola formation, replace Strawberry Crepe with Ion Cookie Robot if the team needs a shield. Try Herb in that slot if another healer would help the group recover. Start with one change and repeat the same stage.

Watch who dies and when. A Cookie that falls before the supports act needs a different answer from a team that survives most of the fight but slowly runs out of healing. Keep Cherry Cola while testing these support changes, rather than removing an attacker and a protector together.

Story difficulty reductions also change how much defense a developed account needs. If the hits no longer threaten the team, an old survival-heavy preset can become unnecessarily slow. Restore damage when the defensive slot has finished its job.

Use the [Cookie tier list](/tier-list/) to compare longer-term investments, but judge a substitution in the fight where you need it. A high-ranked Cookie with weak promotion can still lower the useful strength of your current formation. Check whether the replacement solves the problem that stopped your last run.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="redberry-staircase" title="Redberry: stop chasing and use the stairs">

Redberry is the awkward exception to the default progression team. Close contact can trigger another teleport, leaving your attackers chasing instead of finishing the enemy. More retries with the same approach can repeat the same problem.

Remove Princess Bari and Cherry Cola, then add Tiger Lily and Cool Mint. Treat this as a separate Redberry adjustment; the three complete presets above cover the broader story plan. Watch whether the replacement changes the enemy's movement before judging only the final damage total.

Move toward six or seven o'clock from the opening, as though you are retreating. The useful landmark is the staircase toward the bottom of the arena. **Stop just below the stairs with the enemies above them.** Do not continue all the way to the bottom edge of the map.

Start fighting from that arrangement. The clock direction gets you into the right area, while the staircase tells you where to stop. If the first attempt looks wrong, adjust where you halt rather than changing several Cookies and the route at once. It may take a couple of attempts to stop in the right place.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="story-retry-check" title="Keep the next retry useful">

Before you spend, name the failure. If the formation stays airborne, try the resistance preset. If allies die, test the appropriate shield or healing slot. If everybody survives while the boss barely loses health, inspect Power and damage. If Redberry keeps escaping, change the approach and stopping point.

Save a successful version before moving on. You can keep the ordinary team ready for the next stretch of stages and return to a boss preset only when its mechanic appears again. That saves more effort than rebuilding all twelve slots from memory after every roadblock.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="story-presets-faq" title="Story team FAQ">

### Which team should I try first?

Start with the Bari and Cherry Cola preset for routine story stages. Switch when a specific failure gives you a reason: repeated knock-ups, a GingerCraven damage problem, or Redberry teleporting away from close-range attackers.

### Should I level Candy Shade Pouch before using it?

Try it in the anti-knock-up slot as soon as you have it. The recommendation favors its resistance and HP contribution even at low level. Keep Panda Dumpling as the fallback when you do not own the pouch.

### Is Wind Archer required for GingerCraven?

No. His role in this preset is to help an account raise Power when damage barely lands. Cherry Cola can replace him. Choose the version that works with your promotion and equipment instead of forcing an underbuilt Wind Archer into the team.

### Where should I stop when pulling Redberry south?

Stop just below the southern staircase while the enemies remain above it. Head toward six or seven o'clock from the start, but do not retreat all the way to the arena's bottom edge.

</GuideSection>
