Rye Cookie is easy to dismiss. At low promotion, her skill reads like plain damage: roughly 400% ATK every four seconds with no healing, shield, or fancy debuff attached. That looks thin beside an SSR skill packed with extra effects.

The comparison changes at five stars. Rye gains two additional hits and reaches roughly 600% total damage per cast while keeping the four-second cooldown. She also fires from range and hits enemies ahead, so the skill connects more reliably than a melee attack that can be interrupted halfway through. This is when Rye stops feeling like an inexpensive placeholder and starts carrying stages.

<GuideSection id="five-star-breakpoint" title="Five stars is the real starting line">

Do not judge a one-star Rye against a promoted SSR and call the experiment finished. Her five-star upgrade adds two hits rather than a small side stat. The extra shots turn a modest skill into short-cooldown burst that can clear waves and remove weakened PvP targets.

That promotion is still a real cost. Rye is an SR, so getting copies is more realistic than maxing a premium unit, but it does not happen for free. If she is below five stars, keep her usable rather than pouring every rare Rune material into her. Save the expensive locks until the extra hits exist.

At five stars, adventure stages move faster because the skill returns every four seconds. In PvP, Rye may not lead the total damage chart, but she can delete one target early and turn the rest of the fight into a numbers advantage.

<GuideTip title="Wait for the part that matters">

Use Rye before five stars if your roster needs her, but postpone the expensive build. The promotion changes her skill enough that early damage tests undersell the finished Cookie.

</GuideTip>

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="why-the-skill-works" title="Why the simple skill works">

Rye's skill does not need a paragraph of effects because its timing does the work. A four-second cooldown gives her repeated chances to finish whatever the rest of the team has damaged. The attack reaches enemies ahead too, which lets one cast hit more than the front target.

During adventure waves, Rye contributes steady area damage without walking into melee range. In Arena, she acts more like a finisher. Wind Archer Cookie or Milky Way Cookie can soften several enemies, then Rye's next cast removes the low-health Cookie before a healer recovers them.

Do not read too much into one damage screen. A stronger account can make any lineup look clever. Watch who Rye targets, how often she casts, and whether those casts change the fight. A clean knockout is worth more than padding the total against a tank that never dies.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="orange-multistrike-core" title="Orange Cookie fixes the team around Rye">

Rye receives Multi-strike, and Orange Cookie is the easiest provider to build around. Orange also gives CRIT Chance Up. Her synergy effect lasts ten seconds while her cooldown is shorter than ten seconds, so the buff can stay active through most of the fight.

Rye gets repeated shots and a large CRIT boost without waiting for a narrow burst window. Melon Soda Cookie also receives Multi-strike and fits the same offensive shell. Herb Cookie uses the synergy too, which lets you add serious healing without wasting the provider slot. Macaron Cookie and Space Doughnut's Royal Excellence offer more options when you change modes.

The practical core is Rye plus Orange, then whatever the stage needs. Add Melon Soda for more damage, Herb for healing, or a sturdy frontline when the opener is the problem. Check each Cookie's Granted and Received effects in the [Cookie list](/cookies/) before assuming a shiny unit activates the synergy.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="teams-by-mode" title="Build Rye teams for the mode">

For general PvE, start with Rye, Orange, a frontline, and enough healing to survive the first skill cycle. Pomegranate Cookie, Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician, Cheesecake Cookie, and Macaron Cookie can raise damage through Skill Amp, ATK, or CRIT support. You do not need every buffer at once. Pick the ones that also solve the stage's survival problem.

Single-target bosses need a different approach. Pair Rye with Scorpion Cookie so the team has focused damage after wave clear stops mattering. Rowdy Truck is a useful warning: an adventure lineup that cruises through normal stages can stall there until you remove broad damage and add Scorpion or stronger sustain.

PvP can use Rye beside Wind Archer and Milky Way. The two larger area threats pressure the whole team while Rye finishes exposed targets on her short cooldown. This setup is aggressive. If it loses before Rye's second cast, replace one damage slot with defense instead of adding even more damage.

Use the [Team Builder](/tools/team-builder/) to check your twelve slots rather than copying a lineup with promotions you do not have.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="sugar-rune-stats" title="Best Sugar Rune stats for Rye">

Aim for SR or higher effects once your Rune supply can support several Cookies. Rye has four clean offensive targets:

- ATK% raises every hit.
- CRIT Chance makes the repeated shots convert more often.
- CRIT DMG increases the payoff from Orange Cookie's CRIT support.
- Skill Amp strengthens the cast directly.

Skill Haste is usable, but it is not my first lock. One point gives about 1% cooldown reduction, and Rye already casts every four seconds. Shaving a small fraction from that timer often adds less than a strong ATK or CRIT roll.

Take a good offensive SR before chasing a useless SSR. Rye needs several teammates, and burning the whole account budget on one perfect Rune usually leaves the support core half built. The [gear and Sugar Rune guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-gear-sugar-rune-stats-guide/) has a fuller reroll budget if the lock button keeps winning.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="fire-pets" title="Pets for a Fire damage core">

Rye and Scorpion are both Fire damage dealers, so a focused Pet setup can buff both:

- Sweet n' Sour gives all allies CRIT DMG.
- Floral Froglet gives Fire allies more CRIT DMG.
- Holy Baby Drop gives all allies ATK.

This trio also helps Nameless Cake Hound or Vampire Cookie when they occupy another damage slot. Hot Doggie is a reasonable Skill Amp alternative, while Gold Drop works when the team needs more CRIT Chance. Compare effects on the [Pets list](/pets/) before using a high-rarity Pet that buffs the wrong element.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="bad-matchups" title="When Rye is the wrong answer">

Rye is much less convincing before five stars. She also loses value when a boss demands pure single-target damage, the team cannot keep Multi-strike active, or she dies before cycling the skill.

The failed run tells you what to change. Scorpion covers focused damage. Orange restores the missing synergy. If survival is the problem, remove a greedy attacker for Herb, Milk, or a defensive Cookie instead of rebuilding every slot.

Rye cannot excuse low combat power either. An underpowered team may still suffer a stage penalty. Promotion, levels, gear, and the rest of the lineup have to keep up.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="quick-build" title="Quick Rye Cookie build">

1. Reach five stars before judging Rye's ceiling.
2. Pair her with Orange Cookie for Multi-strike and CRIT Chance Up.
3. Roll ATK%, CRIT Chance, CRIT DMG, or Skill Amp on Sugar Runes.
4. Add Scorpion for strict boss fights and Melon Soda for broader pressure.
5. Use Fire damage Pets when Rye and Scorpion share the team.
6. Replace one attacker with healing or defense if Rye cannot reach her second cast.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="rye-cookie-faq" title="Rye Cookie FAQ">

### Does Rye Cookie need five stars?

Five stars is the important breakpoint because it adds two hits and raises the skill from roughly 400% to 600% total damage per cast. She can fill a slot earlier, but the promoted version is the one worth building around.

### What are the best Sugar Rune stats for Rye Cookie?

Prioritize ATK%, CRIT Chance, CRIT DMG, and Skill Amp. Skill Haste is acceptable, but the four-second base cooldown makes it less urgent than a strong offensive roll.

### Who is Rye Cookie's best teammate?

Orange Cookie is the cleanest partner because she grants Multi-strike and CRIT Chance Up with near-continuous uptime. Scorpion Cookie is the best addition when the team needs more single-target boss damage.

### Is Rye Cookie good in PvP?

Yes, especially as a burst finisher. She can remove damaged enemies quickly, although another Cookie may show more total damage over the full fight.

### Which Pets work with Rye Cookie?

Sweet n' Sour, Floral Froglet, and Holy Baby Drop form a strong Fire damage setup by adding CRIT DMG, Fire ally CRIT DMG, and ATK. Hot Doggie or Gold Drop can replace one slot when the account needs Skill Amp or CRIT Chance.

</GuideSection>
