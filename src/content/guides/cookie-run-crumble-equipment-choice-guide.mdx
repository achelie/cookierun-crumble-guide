Two equipment rolls sit on screen. One adds Combat Power. The other keeps the Accuracy and Focus your team needs. Which one should you keep?

Before roughly Oven level 10, taking the higher-power piece is a reasonable shortcut. After that, check the size of the power gain first. When the two pieces are close, fix missing Accuracy, then consider Focus, Skill Amp, and Skill Haste. Once attacks land reliably, extra Accuracy can wait.

If you keep choosing Accuracy after misses stop, you may pass up the Focus roll that would help your poison stacks land.

<GuideSection id="left-or-right" title="Choose the piece that fixes the current problem">

Use this order for stage progression. It helps with ordinary equipment comparisons; it does not make every small power increase worth a swap.

| What you see | What to favor |
| --- | --- |
| You are still below about Oven level 10 | Higher Combat Power for quick early progress |
| One piece gives a much larger power increase | Consider the power upgrade, then check what useful stats you lose |
| Power is similar and attacks often miss | Accuracy |
| Attacks connect but important debuffs remain unreliable | Focus, alongside a check of your power deficit |
| Accuracy and debuffs already work well | Skill Amp, then Skill Haste as the stage-oriented starting order |
| Your team dies before it can work | Test Damage Reduction instead of forcing another offensive roll |

There is no fixed percentage that makes a power increase "much larger" here. Look at your current stage and the size of the loss elsewhere. A modest upgrade that removes your main Accuracy roll deserves more thought than an obvious early-game replacement.

Check the lineup too. If you are still choosing which Cookies to invest in, use the [Cookie Tier List](/tier-list/) before spending materials around a Cookie you rarely field. Equipment decisions make more sense once you know who will use the stats.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="accuracy-check" title="Fix misses before chasing more damage">

An attack that misses cannot apply its attached debuff. That makes Accuracy more than a damage concern. A missed debuff can leave the rest of the team attacking without the effect it needs.

Open a Cookie's detailed stats and scroll to Accuracy and Focus. Check those numbers, then watch the fight. Frequent "Miss" messages tell you something that the equipment comparison alone cannot.

Around chapter 200, roughly 900 Accuracy is a useful progression reference. Treat it as a place to start checking, not a universal minimum or a cap. Your stage and research affect the comparison. It also says nothing precise about the amount a much earlier account needs.

Suppose the left piece gives a little more power but drops Accuracy, while the right keeps it steady. If misses already interrupt the run, favor the right. If attacks land consistently and Accuracy is comfortably covered, compare the other stats instead. You do not need to keep choosing Accuracy forever.

The [Accuracy and Focus guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-accuracy-focus-guide/) explains the two checks in more detail if you cannot tell which one is failing.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="focus-and-power" title="Judge Focus against the stage, not another account">

Focus has no equally tidy target. Your Combat Power relative to the stage changes how difficult it is to apply debuffs. A stronger account can land them more reliably with less Focus than a weaker account has.

Poison might stall at four or six stacks when you need ten, even though you copied a stronger account's Focus total. More Focus can help, but the power gap still matters.

For a debuff-dependent team, watch the stack count or the effect you need during the same part of the fight. If attacks connect but the effect keeps failing, compare Focus rolls. Avoid judging the swap from one lucky clear. Repeat the same stage with the same lineup so you can see whether the change improves consistency.

You may accept more retries when pushing a single difficult clear. For routine farming, that becomes tiresome fast. A Focus piece that makes the run repeatable can be worth keeping even when another piece looks more aggressive.

Use the [recommended Teams](/teams/) to check whether your formation relies on poison, DEF Down, or other debuffs. A gear choice that helps that formation may matter less in a different one.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="enhancement-order" title="Spread upgrades to +10 before pushing +15">

Bring equipment slots to +10 across the set before concentrating on +15. After that, a practical stage-progression order starts with Accuracy, followed by Focus. Skill Amp is the next default choice, though upgrading the weapon first is another option when damage needs the investment.

Eventually you will work through the remaining equipment too. The question is where the next batch of materials does the most good while you cannot afford everything.

Leave DEF, HP, and Evasion toward the end of this progression order. They give relatively weak power gains in this approach. That does not mean survival never matters. If the team dies early, reassess the fight and test Damage Reduction rather than treating the upgrade list as a command to ignore incoming damage.

Keep enhancement order separate from Sugar Rune choices. They use different decisions and resources. The broader [gear and Sugar Rune guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-gear-sugar-rune-stats-guide/) covers role-specific rolls when you need to tune an individual Cookie beyond this Oven comparison.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="arena-presets" title="Use different priorities for Arena, but check the preset">

For Arena equipment, start with Skill Amp, then Skill Haste. CRIT DMG and CRIT Chance come next, followed by Damage Reduction and CRIT Resistance. This is a practical farming order, not a promise that one stat wins every matchup.

Before spending Dough on a separate Arena setup, check which equipment preset receives the drops. The preset setup described here does not let you freely move a desirable piece from one set to the other. A rare stage upgrade landing in your PvP set can be frustrating when your main set still needs it.

I would finish a useful main set before committing scarce rolls to a second one. If you do build an Arena set, confirm the selected preset before farming and again before fighting.

<GuideTip title="Power suddenly dropped?">

Check the active equipment preset before replacing anything. Switching to a weaker set can explain a sudden power loss even when you have not changed your Cookies.

</GuideTip>

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="eternal-filter" title="Do not make Eternal your only acceptable drop">

Eternal equipment can offer noticeably stronger stat values at comparable enhancement. Its rarity still makes it a poor sole target for an ordinary farming session. You can spend a large pile of Dough waiting for one piece.

Set the farming rarity threshold one grade below the highest grade you can currently obtain. That keeps the next-best drops in consideration while you wait for a better one. Recheck the setting after your Oven unlocks another grade; a filter that made sense earlier may no longer fit.

Higher equipment levels can produce higher stat values, so read the numbers before rejecting a piece by appearance. Compare enhancement levels too. You want to understand what the new roll changes, rather than mistake an enhancement gap for a better underlying piece.

Before your next roll, note your current Accuracy and Focus. If you decide to swap a piece, keep the same lineup and Pets for the next attempt. Watch whether the misses or missing debuffs improve before spending enhancement materials on it.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="equipment-faq" title="Equipment choice FAQ">

### Should I always keep the higher-power equipment?

No. It is a reasonable shortcut before roughly Oven level 10. Later, weigh large power gains against useful stats. When power is similar, fix missing Accuracy before considering Focus, Skill Amp, and Skill Haste.

### Is 900 Accuracy enough for every stage?

No. Roughly 900 Accuracy is a reference around chapter 200, not a universal requirement or cap. Check your current stage, research, and visible misses before deciding whether to add more.

### What Focus number should I aim for?

There is no single target here. Your power relative to the stage affects debuff reliability. Watch the debuff you need and compare Focus upgrades under the same battle conditions.

### Which equipment should reach +15 first?

Bring the set to +10 first. For stage progression, prioritize Accuracy, then Focus, then usually Skill Amp. Weapon-first investment is another option; leave DEF, HP, and Evasion until later unless survival needs attention.

</GuideSection>
