Strawberry Crepe Cookie has a Tank label, so the obvious move is to load their Sugar Runes with HP and Damage Reduction. That obvious move also throws away the reason to use them.

Crepe Crasher is a four-second area attack with pushback. At its highest listed upgrade, the hit reaches 159.2% of ATK with a 2.75 effect radius. Rapid Fire can repeat that hit, and Chargemellow gives the team another Rapid Fire point while raising ATK for its receivers. Strawberry Crepe is less a wall and more a short-range damage dealer wearing the wrong name tag.

A pure defensive page may keep them alive, but it can turn a potential 840,000-damage Arena result into a Cookie that survives long enough to achieve very little. Build damage first, then add one survival line only when the same matchup keeps killing them before a second cast.

<GuideSection id="role-mismatch" title="Stop treating the Tank label as build advice">

Strawberry Crepe pushes enemies away, but the skill does not grant the sort of shield, heal, or large defensive buff that would make them a dependable solo frontline. They still take real punishment at close range. A Tank icon does not cancel that problem.

Their useful loop is simple. Walk into a group, swing the crepe arms, push the pack, then repeat four seconds later. More Rapid Fire activations turn each cast into a much nastier burst. Crowded story waves are the cleanest target because the 2.75 radius can hit several enemies instead of spending the whole animation on one body.

The [CookieRun: Crumble Tier List](/tier-list/) can help you compare investment options, but do not read a letter grade as an order to strip a finished five-star carry. Strawberry Crepe becomes attractive when you already have Pinot Noir, Chargemellow, and enough offensive Runes to support the loop.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="pet-loadout" title="Use the three Pets as one package">

Chargemellow is the piece that changed the build. It grants one Rapid Fire point and raises ATK for Rapid Fire receivers. Strawberry Crepe, Rye, Melon Soda, Macaron, Herb, and Pinot Noir all gain something from the completed package.

Holy Baby Drop raises ATK for the full lineup. That also improves healing that scales with ATK, which makes the Pet less selfish than it looks. Hot Doggie supplies Skill Amp. The stat still improves the damage and eligible buffs that this formation cares about after the stat-debuff correction.

Do not remove Chargemellow because another Pet has more stars. Test what happens to the active synergy first. A lower-star Pet that turns on repeated casts can improve six Cookies at once. The raw Power loss may look ugly while the clear becomes faster.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="sugar-runes" title="Build Sugar Runes for damage">

Start with four stats:

- ATK Amp
- CRIT Chance
- CRIT DMG
- Skill Amp

Those lines improve the job Strawberry Crepe performs in this formation. Skill Haste is acceptable, but the four-second cooldown already cycles quickly. A strong Skill Haste roll is worth keeping. It is not worth discarding a better offensive page to chase one.

Use the [gear and Sugar Rune guide](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-gear-sugar-rune-stats-guide/) before locking an expensive page. You do not need four perfect labels on every Rune. You need enough useful offense that Crepe Crasher hurts every time Rapid Fire repeats it.

Add HP Amp or Damage Reduction only after you see the same survival problem more than once. One defensive line can be sensible in Arena. Turning the whole page into tank stats is a panic response that removes the build's best feature.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="damage-tests" title="Read the damage without fooling yourself">

A clean Arena fight produced about 840,000 damage, close to one million. In a separate close matchup, Strawberry Crepe died early and still matched the damage of a ten-star Milky Way. Those numbers explain the interest, but they do not guarantee the same result on every account.

Target count changes everything. The 2.75 radius loves packed enemies and waves. A lonely boss wastes the area advantage, so a focused single-target attacker may win. Survival changes the result too. Dying before the next four-second cycle turns a great damage page into a short demonstration.

Compare three runs. If Strawberry Crepe lives and the result stays weak, inspect Rapid Fire and the offensive Runes. If they die at the same moment every time, add one defensive line or move back to Dark Choco. If Rye deletes someone at the opener, stop treating that match as a neutral damage test.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="investment" title="Decide whether to invest now">

Build Strawberry Crepe when you need fast AoE clearing, own Pinot Noir, and can field Chargemellow without dismantling another important team. They also make sense when regular pulls have already given you several promotion stars. A forgotten promoted SSR is cheaper to finish than a brand-new premium carry.

Wait when your main damage dealers are still below their important star breakpoints or when Chargemellow would remain too weak to use. Strawberry Crepe fits many formations, but twelve slots fill quickly. Do not copy the top Arena accounts blindly. Those players often counter the same small group of opponents and hide Cookies to protect matchup information.

Save the full AoE preset and build one offensive Rune page. Run the stage that currently blocks you three times. Compare the clear time and note when Strawberry Crepe dies. If the stage gets faster, keep investing. If only the damage report gets prettier while the team still collapses, fix the support package before buying another reroll.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="strawberry-crepe-faq" title="Strawberry Crepe Cookie FAQ">

### Is Strawberry Crepe Cookie a tank or a damage dealer?

They have the Tank role, but this build treats them as an AoE damage dealer. Crepe Crasher provides damage and pushback without enough built-in protection to justify a full defensive Sugar Rune page.

### What are the best Sugar Rune stats for Strawberry Crepe Cookie?

Prioritize ATK Amp, CRIT Chance, CRIT DMG, and Skill Amp. Skill Haste is a useful secondary roll. Add one HP Amp or Damage Reduction line only when they repeatedly die before another cast.

### Which Pets work best with Strawberry Crepe Cookie?

Use Chargemellow, Holy Baby Drop, and Hot Doggie. Chargemellow grants Rapid Fire and ATK to its receivers, Holy Baby Drop raises team ATK, and Hot Doggie adds Skill Amp.

### Is Strawberry Crepe Cookie good in Arena?

Yes, especially against grouped teams that cannot remove or control them early. Rye burst and heavy control can end the run before the damage cycle develops, so test several opponents instead of judging one result.

### Should I use Dark Choco or Devil Cookie?

Dark Choco is the safer DEF Down option. Devil can create a stronger offensive window but dies more easily. Use Devil when the opponent gives the team time, then return to Dark Choco when survival becomes unreliable.

</GuideSection>
