Guild Conquest can make the same account look brilliant in one run and half asleep in the next. Your first attempt might land around 250M damage, then the next jumps by 60M without a promotion or Rune change. That swing is normal. Extra-Stuffed Piñata has enough random targeting and timing variance that one result tells you very little.

Entries are unlimited, so use them. Build a sensible single-target team, keep the important buffs and debuffs, then run it several times. The two lineups below use the same core and the same three Pets. Wind Archer has the better damage ceiling in this comparison, while Milky Way gives accounts without a strong Wind Archer a practical second option.

<GuideSection id="guild-conquest-rules" title="Understand what raises your Guild Conquest score">

Extra-Stuffed Piñata is a Dark boss, which makes Light attackers useful. That does not mean you should cram every Light Cookie into the formation. The boss still asks for enough healing, damage reduction, DEF Down, Lift Resistance, and single-target damage to keep all twelve slots working.

Start by checking the jobs, not the Power total. Scorpion, Rye, and Melon Soda handle most of the direct damage in the two accessible teams. Macaron raises CRIT Chance. Skating Queen adds CRIT DMG. Pomegranate improves eligible skills with Skill Amp Up, while Dark Choco lowers the boss's DEF. If one of those jobs disappears, another damage dealer rarely covers the loss.

Save Guild Medals instead of spending them on whatever looks cheap in the exchange. Tea Knight is built for this mode because his Boss DMG Up applies to the whole plan. You can use the teams below before owning him, then add him once the exchange gives you enough copies.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="cookie-jobs" title="Keep the damage core and support jobs intact">

Scorpion is the center of the damage plan. Five stars matters because her damage over time application becomes much more dependable. Build enough Focus to land it, then add Skill Haste, Skill Amp, and ATK Amp. An all-offense page can post a bigger lucky score, but missed applications make the results messy.

Rye is the burst option. Her five-star breakpoint adds the hits that make the short cooldown worth building around. Melon Soda brings another strong single-target cycle. If one is much weaker, that Cookie becomes the first slot to remove when Tea Knight arrives.

Macaron and Skating Queen are a pair. Macaron raises CRIT Chance, and Skating Queen turns those crits into larger numbers. CRIT Chance does not become dead after 100%. Chance above 100% can produce a double crit, and chance above 200% can produce a triple crit. That is why the formation keeps stacking CRIT instead of stopping at the usual cap.

Pomegranate grants Skill Amp Up. Tiger Lily helps the synergy package. Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician heals and raises ATK. Nameless Cake Hound reduces incoming damage, which prevents an unlucky hit from deleting a damage dealer before the next cast.

Pinot Noir supplies Lift Resistance. Her Healing Received Down does nothing useful to this boss, so build for Focus, Skill Haste, ATK Amp, and the resistance value you need. The full [Pinot Noir build](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-pinot-noir-cookie-build/) explains where that support fits outside this fight.

Dark Choco stays for DEF Down. Devil Cookie can fill the job on some accounts, but Dark Choco is the safer default here. Give him Focus and Skill Haste, plus enough defense to survive. Skill Amp no longer makes this stat debuff stronger.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="sugar-runes" title="Build Sugar Runes around each job">

Damage dealers share a simple starting point: ATK Amp, Skill Amp, and Skill Haste. Add Focus to Scorpion when the damage over time effect misses too often. Wind Archer and Milky Way can stay offensive because they are not carrying the main debuff check.

Supports need a little more judgment:

- Macaron, Pomegranate, Skating Queen, Tiger Lily, and Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician want Skill Haste and Skill Amp because their eligible buffs improve with better casts.
- Pinot Noir wants Focus, Skill Haste, and ATK Amp. Do not spend a fortune trying to improve Healing Received Down with Skill Amp.
- Dark Choco wants Focus and Skill Haste. Add HP or damage reduction if he dies early.
- Nameless Cake Hound wants Skill Haste and HP Amp so the defensive effect stays available.

Do not rebuild twelve Rune pages in one afternoon. Fix Scorpion first, then the weakest support that keeps dying, followed by the better of Rye and Melon Soda. Use the site's [recommended teams](/teams/) to see whether those same Cookies also earn a place in other modes before spending rare rerolls.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="pet-loadout" title="Keep the three-Pet package together">

Use Holy Baby Drop, Hot Doggie, and Panda Dumpling in both formations. Holy Baby Drop raises allied ATK, and Hot Doggie supplies Skill Amp Up. Panda Dumpling completes the anti-lift package beside Pinot Noir, which helps the lineup keep attacking instead of losing casts to displacement.

Copy these three exactly before experimenting. Swapping Panda Dumpling for a prettier damage Pet can look stronger on paper, then lose more time when the formation gets lifted.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="brightseeker-tea-knight" title="Upgrade the team with Brightseeker and Tea Knight">

Brightseeker has the cleanest premium upgrade path because her Light damage matches the Dark boss. Even one copy can beat Wind Archer or Milky Way in this slot. Replace whichever of those two you were using, then keep the rest of the formation stable while you compare scores. The [Brightseeker build](/guides/cookie-run-crumble-brightseeker-cookie-build-team/) covers her offensive Rune priorities and five-star target.

Tea Knight is the next change. Keep Dark Choco. Remove the weaker of Rye and Melon Soda instead, then add Tea Knight for Boss DMG Up. This correction matters because dropping DEF Down can erase much of the gain you expected from the new Cookie.

<GuideTip title="Do not replace Dark Choco first">
Brightseeker takes the Wind Archer or Milky Way slot. Tea Knight takes the weaker Rye or Melon Soda slot. Dark Choco keeps applying DEF Down.
</GuideTip>

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="retry-method" title="Use unlimited retries without wasting upgrade materials">

Save both presets and run each three to five times. Record the best result, not the average of two unlucky openings. If the score swings but nobody dies, the team probably needs more attempts rather than a rebuild. If the same Cookie dies early every time, fix that Rune page or add defense before changing the damage core.

Only change one variable between tests. Swap Wind Archer for Milky Way, run again, then compare. Later, add Brightseeker. When Tea Knight is ready, replace Rye or Melon Soda based on your account. Those extra minutes are cheaper than panic rerolling twelve Rune pages after one bad attempt.

</GuideSection>

<GuideSection id="guild-conquest-faq" title="Guild Conquest team FAQ">

### What is the best CookieRun: Crumble Guild Conquest team without Brightseeker?

Use Wind Archer, Scorpion, Tiger Lily, Pomegranate, Melon Soda, Nameless Cake Hound, Macaron, Rye, Pinot Noir, Skating Queen, Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician, and Dark Choco. Replace Wind Archer with Milky Way if Milky Way has much better investment.

### Which Pets should I use against Extra-Stuffed Piñata?

Use Holy Baby Drop, Hot Doggie, and Panda Dumpling. The loadout adds ATK and Skill Amp while supporting the formation's resistance to lift effects.

### Is Wind Archer or Milky Way better for Guild Conquest?

Wind Archer has the higher result when both have similar investment. A promoted Milky Way with stronger Sugar Runes can still beat an underbuilt Wind Archer, so run both presets several times.

### Who should Brightseeker replace in Guild Conquest?

Brightseeker replaces Wind Archer or Milky Way. If you also add Tea Knight, replace the weaker of Rye and Melon Soda. Keep Dark Choco for DEF Down.

### Why does my Guild Conquest damage change between attempts?

Targeting, crits, debuff applications, and cast timing introduce variance. Entries are unlimited, so repeat a sound lineup several times and use its best score before spending more resources.
